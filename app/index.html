      How this guide was made

      Names, descriptions and grades are the official list as PlayStation publishes it. Every guide is written for this entry, in our own words, from the official description alone — the game is a day old and nothing here was checked in play. Where a description pins no place or step down, the guide says so plainly instead of inventing one.

      Missable flags are inferred from the official descriptions, not confirmed in the game: the two that read like one-off set pieces, and the two that need your partner beside you. The counts (42 Shooting Range challenges, 52 areas of Leonida and the rest) are PSN's progress targets.

      Sources: Exophase — the official trophy list and PSNProfiles — PSN progress targets. The app loads nothing from the network; it runs entirely from its own files.
